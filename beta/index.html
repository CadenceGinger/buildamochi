🎓 The learn room: help us test it

Mochi has a new room past the garden, with a coach on the wall called the Motherboard. You teach Mochi three words there, sit, spin and jump. You use the same Show and Good! buttons as always. The Motherboard tests each word at dawn and dusk and keeps a skills card.

Please read: this is a test

  • Everything we know so far comes from simulations with a simulated owner, not from real players. That is why we need you.
  • In those simulations, with two short lessons a day, the three words stayed distinct. They mostly survived the night and worked at home too, not only in the learn room. But on lesson days Mochi ate much less, so this build has a hunger rule (below).
  • Things may change or break. A test Mochi may be reset when the beta ends.
  • The beta keeps its own separate Mochi in your browser. Your real Mochi at /web/ is not touched.
• ‿ •
The Motherboard is a coach, not a trainer. It never moves, teaches or rewards Mochi. You do the teaching. At dawn and dusk it asks Mochi's brain privately how likely each trick is after the word, after silence and after the other words. Nothing is learned from a test. If Mochi gets too hungry or thirsty (above 80%), it ends the lesson and asks you to carry Mochi home to the bowl. Mochi rarely finds its own way back from the learn room.

▶ Play the learn-room beta

How to play

  1. Pick up Mochi with Carry. Take it through the garden door, then the garden's east door, into the learn room.
  2. Say a word (keys 1–3). Hold its Show button while the word rings, then press Good!.
  3. Keep lessons short, a few per word. Then carry Mochi home to eat and let it sleep.
  4. Check the 🎓 Skills tab after each dawn and dusk.

How to join testing

  1. Play the beta for a few (Mochi) days.
  2. Take a screenshot of your 🎓 Skills card.
  3. Reply to our post on X from @buildamochi with the screenshot. Tell us what surprised you, what felt off, and whether Mochi stayed happy and fed.

𝕏 Open @buildamochi on X

There is no sign-up form and we collect no email. You join by replying on X.

What we count (anonymous)

To see how testing goes overall, the beta sends anonymous totals through GoatCounter. There are no cookies, no names, no ids and nothing per player. Each milestone counts at most once per Mochi per Mochi-day.
