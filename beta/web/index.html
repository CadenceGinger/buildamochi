    Skills · the Motherboard

    The Motherboard lives in the learn room (garden, then the east door). It never moves, teaches or rewards Mochi. You teach there with Show and Good!. At dawn and dusk it asks Mochi's brain in private imagination how likely each word's trick is after the word, after silence and after the other words. Nothing is learned from a test.

    About this copy

    Mochi was created by Bernhard Mueller (@muellerberndt) (Mochi is MIT licensed; this community copy is distributed under GPL-3.0 because it bundles Cadence, which is GPL-3.0): github.com/muellerberndt/mochi. Brain: github.com/muellerberndt/cadence.

    Not an official Pragma Research or Bernhard Mueller release. This copy is hosted by the community. Build your own Mochi · Join the plaza.

    Plaza visitors (opt-in). In the plaza, other Mochis are drawn in your room, and each one is run by its owner's own brain. By default your Mochi's brain never senses them. If you tick notices visitors in the plaza bar (off by default, and only you can switch it), visitors in the same room reach your Mochi's eyes as movement only, with a capped strength. They bring no colour, touch, reward, company or novelty, so nobody can feed, praise or train your Mochi. The marker colour you pick only draws a ring on your own screen and is never sent. Before each plaza visit a backup copy is saved. When you leave, you choose whether to keep what Mochi learned there.

    We count visits anonymously with GoatCounter: totals only, no cookies, no personal data.
