Unofficial community fork. Not an official Pragma Research or Bernhard Mueller release, and not affiliated with them.

- Mochi by Bernhard Mueller, MIT licence: https://github.com/muellerberndt/mochi
- Cadence (the brain), GPL-3.0-only: https://github.com/muellerberndt/cadence
- This embed (embed/index.html, embed.css, bridge.js, coopey-mochi.js) is part of the community copy at
  https://github.com/CadenceGinger/buildamochi and is distributed under GPL-3.0-only, because the page bundles Cadence.
  Complete corresponding source: that repository (the brain pack wheel and host sources ship under web/brains/).

The brain learns only by Cadence online learning while the pet lives; nothing is retrained.
Each visitor's Mochi is stored only in that visitor's browser (IndexedDB); nothing is sent to a server.
